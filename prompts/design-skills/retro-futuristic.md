## Visual rules (intended contract)

- **Dark base + neon accents.** Deep indigo/black with cyan/magenta
  glows; subtle scanline or grid-horizon background.
- **Display type with character.** Wide, slightly condensed geometric or
  techno serif/sans; uppercase tracking on labels.
- **Glow & bevel.** Soft outer glow on key elements; restrained chrome /
  gradient-stroke borders. No flat default buttons.
- **Grid perspective.** A horizon/perspective-grid motif used sparingly
  for depth, not on every section.
- **Motion.** Smooth eased reveals, subtle parallax; no bounce.

## Banned

- Inter / Roboto / system fonts; purple-on-white gradients.
- Overdone glitch on everything (use as a rare accent only).
- Flat material-style components.

## Good for

Tech/SaaS with a bold stance, gaming, audio/synth gear, web3/AI
products, esports, innovation labs.
