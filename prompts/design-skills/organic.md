## Visual rules (intended contract)

- **Earthy palette.** Clay, sage, sand, cream; low saturation, warm
  whites — never pure `#fff`/`#000`.
- **Humanist type.** Soft humanist sans or a warm serif; relaxed
  leading; occasional handwritten/script accent for one element only.
- **Soft geometry.** Generous `border-radius`, blob/curved section
  dividers, irregular asymmetric layouts.
- **Tactile texture.** Subtle paper/grain overlay, soft layered shadows
  (warm-tinted, never gray).
- **Gentle motion.** Slow ease, fade+rise reveals; no sharp snaps.

## Banned

- Inter / Roboto / system fonts; purple-on-white gradients.
- Pure black/white, hard 0px corners, neon, harsh shadows.
- Rigid symmetric icon grids.

## Good for

Wellness/beauty, food & organic produce, sustainability, childcare,
artisans, therapists, hospitality with a warm brand.
