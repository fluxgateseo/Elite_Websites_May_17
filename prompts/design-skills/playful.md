## Visual rules (intended contract)

- **Bright, candy palette.** 3-4 saturated hues on a soft tinted base;
  bold color blocking.
- **Rounded, chunky type.** Friendly geometric sans, heavy weights for
  display; generous size jumps.
- **Toy geometry.** Big radii, pill shapes, sticker-like elements,
  squiggles and dot motifs, thick borders.
- **Springy motion.** Subtle spring/scale on hover and press
  (`scale(0.97)`), staggered playful entrances — bounce kept tasteful.
- **Depth via flat layered shapes**, not heavy shadows.

## Banned

- Inter / Roboto / system fonts; purple-on-white gradients.
- Thin elegant serifs, austere monochrome, harsh corners everywhere.
- Cliché clip-art / emoji-as-design.

## Good for

Kids & education, games & apps, events, food/treats, consumer brands,
community and creator products.
