## Visual rules (intended contract)

- **Dark + metallic.** Deep emerald/navy/black with gold or brass
  accent; high contrast.
- **Geometric display type.** Strong geometric or stylized serif,
  generous tracking, uppercase titles.
- **Ornament as structure.** Symmetric framing, stepped/chevron
  dividers, sunburst and fan motifs, thin double rules — used
  deliberately, not as wallpaper.
- **Symmetry-led layout** (a deliberate counterpoint to the asymmetric
  skills), centered focal compositions.
- **Restrained motion.** Elegant fades and line-draw reveals; no bounce.

## Banned

- Inter / Roboto / system fonts; purple-on-white gradients.
- Rounded soft UI, pastel palettes, casual handwriting.
- Random asymmetry (this style is symmetry-first).

## Good for

Cocktail bars, theatres/cinemas, boutique hotels, jewelry, premium
events, heritage and luxury brands.
