## Visual rules (intended contract)

- **Layered backgrounds.** Stacked gradient meshes + noise + a geometric
  pattern; never a flat fill.
- **Oversized display type.** H1 `clamp(3.5rem, 12vw, 9rem)`, mixed
  weights in one headline, occasional outline/stroke text.
- **Saturated, clashing-by-design palette.** 3+ strong colors, hard
  accents; no timid neutrals as primaries.
- **Grid-breaking.** Overlapping cards, rotated elements, sticker/edge
  bleed, marquee strips.
- **Dense motion.** Orchestrated staggered load (`animation-delay`),
  scroll-reactive elements — high-impact, not random jitter.

## Banned

- Inter / Roboto / system fonts; purple-on-white gradients.
- Symmetric centered hero with one CTA (the AI-slop default).
- Even, evenly-spaced neutral palettes.

## Good for

Festival / events, music & nightlife, bold creative agencies, streetwear
and youth brands, launch/campaign microsites.
