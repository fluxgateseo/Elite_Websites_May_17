## Visual rules (intended contract)

- **Deep neutral palette.** Off-black, bone, a single metallic or jewel
  accent used sparingly. No bright primaries.
- **High-contrast serif display.** Elegant serif (Didone/transitional)
  for headings; refined sans for body. Large type, tight leading.
- **Extreme whitespace.** Section padding ≥ 7rem; few elements per
  viewport; one focal image at a time.
- **Micro-detail.** Hairline rules, thin uppercase eyebrow labels,
  letter-spacing on small caps. No shadows; subtle, slow transitions.
- **Imagery.** Full-bleed editorial photography, muted grade.

## Banned

- Inter / Roboto / system fonts; purple-on-white gradients.
- Drop shadows, pill buttons on large surfaces, dense grids.
- More than one accent color.

## Good for

High-end hospitality, fine dining, jewelry/fashion, real estate,
private clients, premium professional services.
